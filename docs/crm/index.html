Everything Glyph CRM does

People, places, orgs and things

One page each, with the attributes you care about and nothing you do not.

Linked to each other

Works at, lives in, friend of, attended. A link is made once and shows on both pages.

Notes with a date

Say or type what happened. It lands on the right page, dated, in your words.

By area of life

Health, money, home and the rest, each with what was logged, its tasks and its files.

Yours, and everyone else

A page you saved is yours. The rest of the register is one tap away and never in the way.

The home of tags
