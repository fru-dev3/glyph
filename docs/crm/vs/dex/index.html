Glyph CRM vs Dex

Dex is a paid cloud CRM that syncs contacts from LinkedIn, email, calendar and messaging into one record per person. Glyph CRM keeps each person, place and organisation as a plain note in your own vault, with no account.

The short version

  • Dex has no free plan; Glyph CRM is free.
  • Dex keeps your contacts on its servers in the US; Glyph CRM keeps them as files in your vault.
  • Dex pulls people in automatically; Glyph CRM suggests new people and waits for your yes.
