Glyph CRM vs Folk

Folk is a paid cloud CRM for teams, filled from a Chrome extension, email and calendar sync and CSV imports. Glyph CRM is a free personal CRM that keeps each page as a plain note in your own vault.

vs

The short version

  • Folk starts at $24 a user a month; Glyph CRM is free.
  • Folk is built for teams and sales; Glyph CRM is for one person's life.
  • Folk stores data on AWS in Ireland; Glyph CRM stores it in your vault.
