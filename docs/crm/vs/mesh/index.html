Glyph CRM vs Mesh

Mesh, formerly Clay, is a cloud contact app that gathers people from your email, calendar and social accounts and keeps their details current. Glyph CRM keeps people as plain notes in your own vault and adds no one without your yes.

Glyph CRMFree
vs
Mesh$10/mo

The short version

  • Mesh is free up to 1,000 contacts with a card on file; Glyph CRM is free with no account.
  • Mesh fills itself from your accounts; Glyph CRM suggests people and you decide.
  • Mesh keeps data on its servers; Glyph CRM keeps it as files you own.

When to pick Mesh

Pick Mesh if you want contacts filled in and kept current for you from email, calendar and social accounts, on Windows and Android too.

When to pick Glyph CRM

You want a personal CRM whose pages are plain notes in your own vault.
