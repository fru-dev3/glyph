Questions

Is Glyph Life free?

Yes.

Does it need my bank login?

No. It reads what is already in your vault.

Does it cancel subscriptions for me?

No. It asks you to keep or cancel before each date and remembers your answer.

Where is my data kept?

In your own vault. It writes only your decisions.

What do I need?

An Apple silicon Mac on macOS 26 or later; the iPhone app reaches your vault through your own hub. Glyph Native installs it and keeps it up to date, signed and notarized by Apple.

Everything Glyph Life does

What is due, first

The charges and lapses of the coming days, counted in one line at the top.

Keep it or cancel it

Each charge asks for a decision before its date, and remembers the one you gave.

Money out

What your subscriptions cost a month and a year, and the ones with no price yet said as such.

Documents

What has been filed, by kind, with the date each one expires.

Waiting for you

A document the filer could not place asks for a yes, and each yes teaches it a rule.

Cover

Your policies and the day each one ends, so none lapses unnoticed.

Workflows

The recurring jobs, what each watches for, and when it last ran.

Ask it

Ask what is due this week, or say what to cancel.

No new store

It reads what is already in your vault and writes only your decisions.

Mac and iPhone

The Mac reads the vault; the iPhone reads it through your own hub.
