A free, private alternative to Fireflies

Fireflies records meetings through a bot or its desktop app and works in its cloud. Glyph Jot records with no bot, offline, on your Mac, for free.

What Fireflies does

  • Fireflies records meetings by inviting its notetaker, Fred, into the call, or through its desktop app capturing the computer's audio.1
  • It transcribes and summarizes in its cloud and keeps meetings on its servers.2

What Fireflies costs

  • Free: 400 minutes of storage per team and up to 2 hours per meeting. No downloads.3
  • Pro: $18 a seat a month, or $10 a seat a month billed yearly.4
  • Business: $29 a seat a month, or $19 billed yearly.4

US prices from Fireflies's own pages, checked November 27, 2026. Prices change; the links show today's.

What Glyph Jot does

  • Hold Fn and talk, in any app on the Mac. Let go and the words are typed where your cursor is.
  • Fn + Shift saves what you said as a note instead of typing it.
  • Click Record for meetings: Zoom, Meet, FaceTime or a talk in the room. No bot joins. The transcript, summary and action items are written on your Mac.
  • Speech is turned into text by Apple's on-device model. Summaries and clean-up use Apple Intelligence on the Mac. Glyph Jot makes no network requests and needs no account.
  • Everything is plain files in a folder you choose: notes are Markdown, dictations are lines of JSON, recordings are folders with an .m4a file.
  • Free, with no paid tier and no word or minute limit.

Where Jot is behind

  • Jot is for the Mac, with an iPhone app and keyboard in TestFlight testing. There is no Windows, Android or web version.
  • Jot listens in one speech language at a time, picked in Settings. It does not detect the language as you talk.
  • Meetings stay on your Mac. There are no share links, team workspaces or app integrations.
  • Jot does not watch your calendar or join meetings for you. You click Record when the call starts.

Bring your notes over

  • Open a meeting in Fireflies, click Download, choose Transcript and a format: PDF, DOCX, SRT, CSV, JSON or MD. Downloads need Pro or higher.5
  • Choose MD and save the files into Jot's notes folder.
  • Jot lists every Markdown (.md) file in its notes folder as a note. The notes folder is the one you pick in Settings; in a Glyph vault it is data/domains/notes/source/jot.
  • Recordings come over too: Import Audio (Shift-Command-I, or drop files on the library) takes .m4a, .mp3, .wav, .aiff and video files and transcribes them on your Mac.

What you need

An Apple silicon Mac on macOS 26 or later. Summaries and asking your history need Apple Intelligence; plain dictation and recording do not. Glyph Native installs Glyph Jot and keeps it up to date.
