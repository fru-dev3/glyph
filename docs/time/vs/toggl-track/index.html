Glyph Time vs Toggl Track

Toggl Track keeps your time in its cloud, behind an account. Glyph Time keeps it in plain files on your Mac.

Glyph TimeFree
vs
Toggl Track$12/mo

The short version

  • Toggl Starter is $12 a user a month. Glyph Time is free.
  • Toggl needs an account and keeps your time in its cloud. Time needs neither.
  • Toggl runs on every platform. Time is Mac only, iPhone in testing.
