Glyph Time vs Timing

Timing tracks every app, document and website automatically, for a monthly fee. Glyph Time is a count-up you start, free and open source.

The short version

  • Timing has no free plan. Glyph Time is free.
  • Timing records apps, documents and sites on its own. Time keeps only the project and the app in front.
  • Both keep your time on your device by default.
