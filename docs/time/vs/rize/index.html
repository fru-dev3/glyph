Glyph Time vs Rize

Rize watches your activity and keeps it in its cloud for AI to sort. Glyph Time keeps only the project and the app in front, on your Mac.

The short version

  • Rize Basic is $12.99 a month. Glyph Time is free.
  • Rize keeps activity in its cloud and trains its own models on de-identified data. Time keeps its files on your Mac.
  • Rize sorts work with AI. Time guesses the project from the app in front, and you can rename it.
