Everything Glyph Ibis does

What the world knows

Glyph Jot keeps what you know. Glyph Ibis brings in what the world knows: funding rounds, releases, leaders, lawsuits, breaches, earnings, regulations and more, on AI, data and tech.

50+ context sources

Each source is its own small site, like funding.fru.dev or releases.fru.dev, kept current by its own agent on its own schedule. context.fru.dev gathers them all.

Today

One page for the day: a short brief, what is coming up, and every story ranked by priority, with the companies in it and the sources it came from.

For your agents

Point an agent at context.fru.dev/llms.txt once. It finds the right source, reads it and links what it used. There is an MCP server and an OpenAPI description too.

Public and linked

Every entry comes from public sources and links to them, so you and your agents can check it.

Why Ibis

Named for the ibis, the bird of Thoth, the Egyptian god of knowledge and of writing: the glyphs the family is named for. For two days in October 2026 it was called Glyph Knowledge. Same sites, same addresses: context.fru.dev and every source keep their links.
