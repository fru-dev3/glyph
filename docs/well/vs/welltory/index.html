Glyph Well vs Welltory

Welltory measures heart rate variability with your phone camera or a smartwatch and turns it into stress and energy scores on its own US servers. Glyph Well records what you say about your day in your own vault and shows how often things followed each other.

The short version

  • Welltory centres on heart rate variability. Well centres on your own words, with Garmin and Apple Health beside them.
  • Welltory needs an account and stores data mainly in the US. Well has no account.
  • Welltory uses anonymized AI chats to improve its own models and shares ad IDs in the US. Well does neither.
