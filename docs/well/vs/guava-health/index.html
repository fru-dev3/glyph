Glyph Well vs Guava Health

Guava Health is a cloud health record: you log symptoms and medications, pull in patient portal records and share a summary with your doctor. Glyph Well keeps your log and lab results as files in your own vault and makes the visit summary PDF on your device.

The short version

  • Guava keeps your records in its HIPAA-compliant cloud. Well keeps them in your vault.
  • Guava's AI assistant runs on OpenAI and costs $8 a month. Well is free.
  • Guava can import patient portal records. Well takes lab results you add yourself.

When to pick Guava Health

Pick Guava Health if you want your patient portal records pulled into one place, on the web as well as phones, and family caregivers on one profile.

When to pick Glyph Well

You want to log health by talking, see your own patterns, and keep it all in files you own.
