Glyph Well vs Bearable

Bearable is a phone app where you tap to rate symptoms, mood and habits, and it backs your data up to its own servers. Glyph Well lets you say how your day went on a Mac or iPhone and keeps every entry as a line in a file in your own vault.

The short version

  • Bearable logs by taps on iOS and Android. Well logs by talking, on Mac and iPhone.
  • Bearable backs data up to servers in Frankfurt. Well keeps it in your vault, with no account.
  • Bearable puts correlation reports and history past 30 days behind its subscription. Well is free.

When to pick Bearable

Pick Bearable if you want a mature Android or iPhone tracker with 30+ reports, a large community and a sliding-scale program for people who cannot pay.

When to pick Glyph Well

You want to log health by talking, see your own patterns, and keep it all in files you own.
