Glyph Well vs Exist

Exist pulls data from services like Apple Health, Garmin and Oura into its own servers, adds mood and tags you enter, and finds correlations. Glyph Well reads Garmin and Apple Health beside the words you speak and keeps all of it in your own vault.

The short version

  • Exist costs $6.99 a month after a 30-day trial. Well is free.
  • Exist logs by scores and tags. Well logs by talking.
  • Exist keeps your data on its hosted servers with an account. Well has no account.

When to pick Exist

Pick Exist if you want many services synced in one place (Garmin, Oura, Withings, Strava, calendar and more), a web app, and experiments to test a hunch.

When to pick Glyph Well

You want to log health by talking, see your own patterns, and keep it all in files you own.
