Everything Glyph Well does

Say it

Talk or type a sentence and it becomes entries: the meal, the symptom, the sleep, each on the day it happened.

Saved as you say it

Nothing waits behind a Save button. Undo takes back what one sentence became.

What often came before

Counts of your own days: how often a symptom followed a food, a short night or a hard workout. Never a cause.

Bowel movements in one tap

The seven Bristol types as plain shapes, with colour, urgency and the rest when you want them.

Lab results against their range

Each marker over time, by marker or by the place that ran it.

A summary for the visit

Days logged, symptoms, medicines and results for the period, as one page or a PDF.

Questions for the appointment

Say them as they occur to you and tick them off in the room.

Your wearable beside your words

Sleep, heart rate, steps and workouts from Garmin and Apple Health, next to what you said.

Research from your own files

Ask about your own entries and results. It does not search the internet and gives no advice.

Plain files, your vault

Every entry is a line in a file you own. fru.dev does not collect it and cannot read it.
