Everything Glyph Studio does

A lead agent and specialists

Your lead agent answers first, then specialists weigh in on the same question.

Convene a council

Several models answer one decision at once; a chair model reads them all and writes the verdict, with where they disagreed.

Your compass

Your purpose and values, written once. Every suggestion is checked against them.

Domains with memory

Health, money, work, home: each area of your life keeps its own context and memory, and can use its own AI model.

People, things and projects

Entities, events and projects, all linked, so the agents know what you mean.

Arena benchmarks

Score models on your own questions before you trust them with a decision.

Every AI, switch anytime

Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or local models through Ollama. Your memory stays put when you switch.

Bunker or cloud

Bunker Mode runs entirely on local models, so nothing leaves your machine. Cloud Mode brings in the frontier models you pick.

Plain files you own

Your vault is a folder of plain files on your machine. Open it, back it up, take it anywhere. Glyph Jot and Glyph Record can fill it.

Mac, Windows or the terminal

A desktop app for Mac and Windows, and a CLI for macOS, Linux and WSL, on the same vault.

No telemetry by default

Glyph Studio sends nothing unless you turn it on, and you can see exactly what is sent.

GPL-3.0. Read every line on GitHub.
