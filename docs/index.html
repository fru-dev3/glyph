Catch it

Glyph Dictate types what you say into whatever app you are in: hold Fn on the Mac, or use the Glyph keyboard on iPhone. Glyph Jot keeps a thought as a note: in the Glyph app on iPhone, or Fn + Shift on the Mac. Glyph Record will keep your meetings and long conversations, filed by who was there: Mac first, iPhone later.

The iPhone app

On iPhone it is one app, called Glyph: it holds Jot, brings the Glyph keyboard for Dictate, and later Record. It is in TestFlight testing.

Keep it

Everything lands as plain files you can open, back up and move: Markdown, text and audio. No account, and the speech runs on your own devices.

Work on it

Glyph Studio reads that vault, area by area: health, money, work, home. Ask one model, or a council of several, and pick a different model for each area.

Glyph Code

Glyph Code is for people who run coding agents: Claude Code, Codex, Gemini and Cursor sessions each get one name, so you always know which is which.

Why Glyph

Glyph is short for petroglyph. Every icon in the family carries the same figure, with one sign beside it for what that app does.
