01What is Glyph?

A family of apps for your agentic life, made by fru.dev: Glyph Bot, Jot, Ibis and Rig, and the tools around them.

02Is it free?

Yes. Every Glyph app is free.

03Where is my data?

In files you own: your Mac, or a vault folder you choose. fru.dev runs no server for the apps.

04Is it open source?

Bot, Jot, Rig, Arena, Cast and Time are open source under the MIT license. The newer apps are private for now.

05How do I install the apps?

Install Glyph Native. It installs each app and keeps it up to date, signed and notarized by Apple.

06What do I need?

An Apple silicon Mac; most apps need macOS 26 or later. iPhone apps are in testing.

07Can you set it up for me?

Yes. One hour, live on your Mac, with the builder of Glyph: the apps, Jot dictation and meetings, and your AI agents on your own files. $149, and you pick the time right after paying.

08How can I support Glyph?

Every app stays free. If Glyph saves you a subscription, you can support it monthly or once at glyph.fru.dev/support. Nothing is locked.

Four layers, plus three tools

  1. 1Glyph BotOne harness that builds, runs and watches your agents, with your whole vault behind them.
  2. 2Glyph JotDictation that types anywhere on your Mac and iPhone, meetings recorded and transcribed, every word kept in a vault you own. Unlimited, and free.
  3. 3Glyph IbisFifty live sources of outside knowledge, refreshed daily into your vault, so your agents know today instead of their training cutoff.
  4. 4Glyph RigA harness over your harnesses. Names, connects and runs agents across every Mac you own.

Bot builds and runs a harness; the Rig connects and manages the harnesses across machines.

Glyph ArenaRuns the same job across every model and recipe, scored on your own work, and tells you which one actually won. Glyph CastRecord your screen, get a clean video: voice cleaned, ums cut, framed and zoomed. Glyph TimeA quiet count-up at the edge of the screen, no timer to start, every session saved to your vault as a plain line.

Context compounds

What you jot and what the world publishes both land as context your agents can read. Every note and every source makes the next answer better, on any model.

Your files

Jot and Bot keep everything as plain files on your computer: Markdown, text and audio. No account, and the speech runs on your own devices.

Bot and the Rig

Bot is one harness: you ask, it works on your vault with one model or a council. The Rig sits under every harness you run, Claude Code, Codex, Gemini or Cursor, and names, connects and manages them across your Macs.

Plus three tools

Glyph Arena tests which model and recipe does each task best on your own work, and Bot follows its picks. Glyph Cast turns screen recordings into clean videos. Glyph Time shows where your hours go.

Formerly

Bot was Glyph Studio and Prevail. Jot took in Dictate and Record (Memosa). Ibis kept its name, as Glyph Ibis. The Rig was Glyph Code: same glyph command, same repo.

Why Glyph

Glyph is short for petroglyph. Every icon in the family carries the same figure, with one sign beside it for what that part does.
