Context compounds

What you jot and what the world publishes both land as context your agents can read. Every note and every source makes the next answer better, on any model.

Your files

Jot and Bot keep everything as plain files on your computer: Markdown, text and audio. No account, and the speech runs on your own devices.

Bot and the Rig

Bot is one harness: you ask, it works on your vault with one model or a council. The Rig sits under every harness you run, Claude Code, Codex, Gemini or Cursor, and names, connects and manages them across your Macs.

Plus three tools

Glyph Arena tests which model and recipe does each task best on your own work, and Bot follows its picks. Glyph Cast turns screen recordings into clean videos. Glyph Time shows where your hours go.

Formerly

Bot was Glyph Studio and Prevail. Jot took in Dictate and Record (Memosa). Ibis kept its name, as Glyph Ibis. The Rig was Glyph Code: same glyph command, same repo.
