Everything Glyph Mobile does

Tap to talk

The words appear while you speak, with Apple's on-device speech model. Nothing leaves the phone.

The keyboard

Switch to the Glyph keyboard in any app, tap the mic and talk: the words are typed at the cursor, and each dictation is also kept as a memo.

Plain files

Each memo is an audio file or a photo plus a text file, in the Files app and in Finder over the cable.

Tags, not folders

A memo can carry several tags. A memo that starts with "Task", "Idea" or "Prompt" is tagged for you.

Cleaned up

Ums and ahs are taken out, "no wait" corrections and spoken lists are handled on the phone.

Photos and notes

Take a photo or pick a screenshot, or type a quick note. Share anything to it from the Share sheet.

Copy what's new

Home shows the memos you haven't copied yet. Copy them all at once, with dates and photos, into Google Docs, Notes or Mail.
