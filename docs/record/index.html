What Glyph Record will do

Any call, no bot

Zoom, Google Meet, FaceTime, Slack or Discord, or a conversation in the room through the mic. Nothing joins your call.

Filed for you

Each conversation is filed by the people in it, their companies and your #tags, so you find it by who, not by date.

Summary and action items

A short summary and the action items, written when the call ends.

On the Mac, start to finish

Apple's speech recognition writes the transcript and Apple's on-device model writes the summary. Nothing leaves the Mac.

Plain files

Audio, transcript and notes stay as files in a folder you choose, ready for Glyph Studio or any AI you use.
