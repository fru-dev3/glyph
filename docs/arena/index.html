01Is Glyph Arena free?

Yes. Glyph Native installs it and keeps it up to date, and the engine that runs every test is open source (GPL-3.0). Cloud model calls are billed by their providers, under a monthly cap you set.

02Which models can it test?

Every model your installed tools reach: Claude Code, Codex, the Gemini CLI, Ollama on this Mac and your other Macs, and image and voice models through the API keys you already have. Each effort level can enter on its own.

03What do Clear, Leaning and Coin flip mean?

Two models are compared on the tests both took. Clear means the record would almost never happen by chance (six straight wins or better). Leaning means more tests could settle it. Coin flip means nothing separates them yet.

04Are health and money questions sent to the cloud?

No. Those areas run only on models on your own Macs, refused before any call.

05What if two models keep tying?

When both score near the top, the tests are too easy. Arena says so and asks for a harder test, instead of running more of the same.

06Can you set it up for me?

Yes. One hour, live on your Mac, with the builder of Glyph: the apps, Jot dictation and meetings, and your AI agents on your own files. $149, and you pick the time right after paying.

07How can I support Glyph?

Every app stays free. If Glyph saves you a subscription, you can support it monthly or once at glyph.fru.dev/support. Nothing is locked.

Everything Glyph Arena does

Every model you can reach

Claude Code, Codex, the Gemini CLI, Ollama on this Mac and your other Macs, and image and voice models through your API keys. Each effort level can enter on its own.

Tests from your own work

23 areas in four groups (Make, Build, Life, Think), with tests drawn from your notes and a rubric for each. Add a test or a whole area in a line.

Head to head, test by test

Two models are compared on the tests both took, so each test's difficulty cancels out. The record gives Clear, Leaning or Coin flip.

Never crowned on too few

Under five scored tests an area says Too early to call, with Run the rest. A model that met no one is not placed.

Best, everyday and cheapest

Each area names the best model, a quicker one and a cheaper one, and those two must not trail the best at all.

Ask

Type what you are doing (a tax question, a SwiftUI bug) and get the model to use, the command that starts it, and who has not been tested yet.

Settle it

When a lead is not clear, four more tests for the two at the top, a number fixed before the result.

Says when tests are too easy

When two models both score near the top and keep tying, Arena asks for a harder test instead of more of the same.

Compare any two

Two models side by side: the areas each is ahead in, then any area test by test, with both answers.

Pictures, clips and voices side by side

Image, video and voice areas show every model's take next to the others.

Health and money stay home

Those areas run only on models on your own Macs, refused before any call. Nothing is sent.

A spend cap

A monthly cap is checked before every cloud call; answers already given come from the cache, free.

Open engine

The engine that runs every test is Glyph Engine, open source under GPL-3.0.
