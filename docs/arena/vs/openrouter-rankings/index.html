Glyph Arena vs OpenRouter rankings

OpenRouter ranks models by how many tokens apps send through its API, which shows popularity, not answer quality. Glyph Arena judges answers on your own tasks and turns the results into a routing policy.

The short version

  • OpenRouter counts tokens. Glyph Arena judges answers.
  • OpenRouter reflects everyone's apps. Glyph Arena reflects your tasks.
  • OpenRouter routes requests for a 5.5% credit fee. Glyph Arena routes through tools you already have.
