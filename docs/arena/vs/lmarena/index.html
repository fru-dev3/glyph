Glyph Arena vs LMArena

LMArena ranks models from anonymous side-by-side votes on prompts the public types in. Glyph Arena replays your own past tasks on two recipes at a time and writes a routing policy for your work.

The short version

  • LMArena ranks models on everyone's prompts. Glyph Arena tests on your own tasks.
  • LMArena may share de-identified chats with researchers and model providers. Glyph Arena keeps health and money tasks on local models.
  • LMArena gives a public leaderboard. Glyph Arena gives a routing policy.
