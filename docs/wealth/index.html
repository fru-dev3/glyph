Questions

Is Glyph Wealth free?

Yes.

Does it need my bank login?

No. Nothing here holds a password to your bank. Numbers arrive by voice, by a statement file or by confirming a note.

Where is my data kept?

As plain lines in whole cents in your own vault. fru.dev does not collect it.

What does it never invents a number mean?

Every figure traces to a line you said or a file you brought in. What is not known says so and is never shown as a zero.

Is it financial advice?

No. It does arithmetic on the numbers you give it.

What do I need?

An Apple silicon Mac on macOS 26 or later; the iPhone app reaches your vault through your own hub. Glyph Native installs it and keeps it up to date, signed and notarized by Apple.

Everything Glyph Wealth does

It never invents a number

Every figure traces to a line you said or a file you brought in. What is not known says so, and is never shown as a zero.

Three kinds of certainty

Read from a statement, sourced from a note, or claimed. Totals add only the first two; an estimate is shown beside them, outside the figure.

Say it

Say a balance, a spend or a transfer in plain words. It reads the amount, the account and the date, and keeps your words beside what it understood.

Bring in a statement

CSV, OFX and QFX files. It shows the rows it read, the ones it already had and the ones it skipped, and says so when a file cannot be read.

No bank login

Nothing here holds a password to your bank. Numbers arrive by voice, by a file, or by confirming what a note in your vault already says.

Cash flow

What a month costs, how much of it does not stop, what was kept, and the charges that come back.

Portfolio

What is invested, the fees it costs each year and over ten, and when more than a fifth sits in one holding.

Scenarios

How many months the money lasts if work stopped, the number you are aiming at, and the year it is reached on a poor, a normal and a good run.

Papers and dates

The documents a tax year usually needs, ticked off as they arrive, and the dates that do not move.

Plain lines in your vault

Whole cents, one line per fact, append only. Each device writes its own file, so a synced folder never conflicts.

Tags

The same tags as the rest of the Glyph family, read from one register in your vault.

Mac and iPhone

The same screens on both, saved to the same vault.
