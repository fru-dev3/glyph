Glyph Wealth vs Copilot Money

Copilot is a paid Apple-first app that links over 16,000 institutions through Plaid and Finicity and stores data in the United States. Glyph Wealth is free, links no bank, and keeps your numbers in your own vault.

The short version

  • Copilot connects to your banks. Glyph Wealth needs no bank login.
  • Copilot costs $109 a year. Glyph Wealth is free.
  • Copilot keeps data on its US servers. Glyph Wealth keeps plain lines in files you own.
