Glyph Wealth vs Monarch Money

Monarch is a paid service that links your bank accounts through aggregators and keeps your data in your Monarch account. Glyph Wealth is free, needs no bank login or account, and keeps every number as plain lines in your own vault.

The short version

  • Monarch syncs balances from your banks. Glyph Wealth takes numbers by voice, statement file or a confirmed note.
  • Monarch costs $99.99 a year. Glyph Wealth is free.
  • Monarch needs an account. Glyph Wealth writes files you own.
