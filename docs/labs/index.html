Glyph Labs

Apps made for one life. Yours.

Free, open source and Agent Ready. Everything stays in files you own.

  • Glyph JotWhat you know: capture notes, ideas, decisions and meetings as your personal context.
  • Glyph CastScreen videos, cleaned up: record your screen, get a clean MP4 with the voice cleaned, ums cut, framed and zoomed.
  • Glyph TimeSee where your hours go: a quiet count-up on the edge of your screen, a nudge every 30 minutes, every session saved to your vault.
  • Glyph IbisWhat the world knows: live data and outside knowledge for your agents, from 50+ context sources.
  • Glyph RigWhere your agents run: one Rig that names, connects and manages every harness across your Macs.
  • Glyph ArenaBot's lab: finds the best model and recipe for each task, tested on your own work.
  • Glyph NativeOne install for every Glyph app: Bot, Jot, Arena, Cast and Time, installed and kept up to date.
