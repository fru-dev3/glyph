Glyph Connect vs Composio

Composio is a hosted service that connects agents to apps through its SDKs and MCP, with sign-in handled on its side. Glyph Connect is a Mac and iPhone app that shows the tools your agents can already reach, from the app registry in your own vault.

The short version

  • Composio is a developer platform with an API key; Glyph Connect is an app with no account.
  • Composio runs your tool calls on its servers; Glyph Connect reads what is on your machine.
  • Both can limit single tools and mark read-only ones.

When to pick Composio

Pick Composio if you are building an agent product and want hundreds of app connections and sign-in handled for you.

When to pick Glyph Connect

You want to see and switch, per tool, what your agents may read and write.
