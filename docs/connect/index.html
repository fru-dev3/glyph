Everything Glyph Connect does

One number first

How many of your apps your agents can reach right now, and which ones need you.

Needs you

A sign-in that lapsed or a key that is missing is named, with the step that fixes it.

Every connection

Open, off, failing or not connected, filtered by kind and by area of life.

What each tool may do

Reads and writes are listed apart. A write changes something outside your vault and says so.

Cut one tool

Switch a single tool off in chat or in sync and leave the rest of the app working.

Or the whole app

One switch says whether agents may reach an app at all. It is instant and it is reversible.

No write runs unattended by accident

One click clears every write tool from sync, and one puts them back.

Ask it

Ask what can be reached, or say what to cut off, and it does it with an Undo.

Your registry, not a new one

It reads the app registry already in your vault and changes only the keys it owns.

Mac and iPhone

The Mac reads the vault; the iPhone reads it through your own hub.
