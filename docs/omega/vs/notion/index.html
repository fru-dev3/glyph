Glyph Omega vs Notion

Notion is a cloud workspace of pages and databases; goals and weekly reviews come from templates you build or install. Glyph Omega has mission, values, goals and the weekly review built in, as plain files in your vault.

Glyph OmegaFree
vs
Notion$10

The short version

  • Notion is a blank workspace you shape. Omega is built for one job: goals down to next actions.
  • Notion stores workspaces on AWS, in the US by default. Omega keeps files in your vault.
  • Notion offline needs pages downloaded first on the free plan. Omega files are always on your device.

When to pick Notion

Pick Notion if you want one flexible workspace for notes, docs and databases, shared with a team, and are happy to build or pick a goals template.

When to pick Glyph Omega

You want goals broken down to the next action, kept in files you own.
