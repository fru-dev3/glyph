Everything Glyph Omega does

One north star

Your purpose in a sentence, at the top of Today and above everything else.

Mission, vision and values

Written once, in your words, with the values in the order you would keep them.

Goals by area of life

Health, money, learning and the rest, each goal with a due date, a measure and its progress.

Broken down to the next move

A goal is not finished being written until it has a next action. One that has none says so.

Talk through your day

Say where things stand. It proposes what changed on which goal and you confirm before anything is saved.

A review once a week

One goal at a time: move the progress, say how it is going, make sure there is a next action.

Your words are kept

What you said is saved first, word for word, before anything reads it.

Plain files in your vault

The same files your agents read. Nothing is stored anywhere else.

When the vault is away

It shows the last copy it read, says when that was, and writes nothing until the vault is back.

Tags

The same tags as the rest of the Glyph family, read from one register in your vault.

Mac and iPhone

The same goals on both, through your own hub.
