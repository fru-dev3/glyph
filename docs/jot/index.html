Everything Glyph Jot does

One job

Talk, and your words land where you want them: typed into the app you are in, or kept as a note. On the Mac and on iPhone.

Mac: hold Fn to type

Hold Fn, say what you want, and let go. The words are typed wherever your cursor is: email, chat, documents, forms, the terminal, any app.

Mac: Fn + Shift for a note

Hold Fn + Shift instead and nothing is typed: the thought is saved as a Markdown note in the folder you choose, an Obsidian vault too.

iPhone: the Glyph app

Open the Glyph app, tap and talk. The words appear while you speak, with Apple's on-device speech model, and the thought is kept as a note with its tags. In TestFlight testing.

iPhone: the Glyph keyboard

Switch to the Glyph keyboard in any app, tap the mic and talk, and the words are typed at the cursor. Every word is kept as a note too.

Hands-free

Double-press the key and keep talking for as long as you like. Press it once more to stop. Fn by default, or Right Option, Right Command or Right Control.

Cleans up as you talk

Ums, ahs and false starts are taken out, and "Tuesday, no wait, Thursday" types only Thursday. Long rambles are tidied while you are still talking.

A style for each app

Formal in Mail, relaxed in Slack and Messages, exact in the terminal: say "dash dash", "slash" or "dot" and get the symbols, end with "send it" and it presses Return.

Learns your words

Fix a name or a term it got wrong, and it spells it right from then on. Add your own words in the Dictionary.

Fill a form, rewrite a paragraph

Say "Fill the form, name Sam Lee, city Austin" and every field is filled; password fields are left alone. Select text and say "make it shorter" to rewrite it.

Notes as plain files

Each note is a text file, with the audio or a photo beside it. A note can carry several tags; one that starts with "Task", "Idea" or "Prompt" is tagged for you.

Ask what you said

"When is the launch now?" It answers from your own dictations and notes, on your Mac.

Private by design

Speech and clean-up run on your own devices with Apple's models. No account, no audio sent anywhere, and it never types into a password field.

Needs

On Mac, an Apple silicon Mac on macOS 26 or later; free and open source. On iPhone, the Glyph app, in TestFlight testing and not on the App Store yet.
