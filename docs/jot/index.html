Everything Glyph Jot does

Two jobs, one app

Hold Fn to jot: your words are typed into the app you are in. Click Record for meetings and long conversations. Everything lands in one library on your Mac. Glyph Record is now part of Glyph Jot.

Hold Fn to type

Hold Fn, say what you want, and let go. The words are typed wherever your cursor is: email, chat, documents, forms, the terminal, any app.

Fn + Shift for a note

Hold Fn + Shift instead and nothing is typed: the thought is saved as a note in your library.

Click Record for meetings

Zoom, Google Meet, FaceTime, Slack or Discord, or a talk in the room. It records your mic and the Mac's sound, with no bot joining the call, then writes the transcript, a summary and the action items.

Jot while you record

Hold Fn during a meeting and it types where your cursor is; that stretch is kept out of the meeting's transcript and summary. Fn + Shift saves a note linked to the meeting at that moment.

One library

Notes, dictations and recordings in one place, filed by People, Accounts and tags, so you find a call by who was there, not by date.

Cleans up as you talk

Ums, ahs and false starts are taken out, and "Tuesday, no wait, Thursday" types only Thursday. Formal in Mail, relaxed in Slack, exact in the terminal.

Learns your words

Fix a name or a term it got wrong, and it spells it right from then on. Add your own words in the Dictionary.

Ask what you said

"When is the launch now?" It answers from your own notes, dictations and meetings, on your Mac.

Private by design

Speech, transcripts and summaries run on your Mac with Apple's on-device models. No account, no audio sent anywhere, and it never types into a password field.

iPhone

The Glyph app and the Glyph keyboard on iPhone, in TestFlight testing and not on the App Store yet.

Needs

An Apple silicon Mac on macOS 26 or later. Free and open source on GitHub.
