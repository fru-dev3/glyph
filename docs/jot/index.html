01Is Glyph Jot really free?

Yes. There is no paid tier, no subscription and no word or minute limit. The code is open on GitHub under the MIT license.

02Does my voice leave my Mac?

No. Speech is turned into text by Apple's on-device model, and summaries and clean-up use Apple Intelligence on the Mac. Glyph Jot makes no network requests.

03Does a bot join my calls?

No. Click Record and it records your microphone and the Mac's sound. Nobody in the call sees a notetaker join.

04Should I tell people I am recording?

Yes. Follow the consent rules where you and the other people are. Glyph Jot records what your Mac hears; asking first is up to you.

05Does it work offline?

Yes. Dictation, recording, transcripts and summaries all run on the Mac, with or without a connection.

06Where are my notes kept?

In a folder on your Mac that you choose, an Obsidian vault too. Notes are Markdown files, dictations are lines of JSON, and recordings are plain folders with an .m4a file.

07What do I need?

An Apple silicon Mac on macOS 26 or later. Summaries, Command Mode and asking your history need Apple Intelligence; plain dictation and recording do not.

08Is there an iPhone app?

The Glyph app and the Glyph keyboard are in TestFlight testing, not on the App Store yet.

09How is it different from Wispr Flow or Granola?

They work in their cloud and need an account. Glyph Jot does dictation and meetings in one app, on your Mac, with no account. See Compare for each app side by side, with sources.

10Can you set it up for me?

Yes. One hour, live on your Mac, with the builder of Glyph: the apps, Jot dictation and meetings, and your AI agents on your own files. $149, and you pick the time right after paying.

11How can I support Glyph?

Every app stays free. If Glyph saves you a subscription, you can support it monthly or once at glyph.fru.dev/support. Nothing is locked.

Everything Glyph Jot does

Two jobs, one app

Hold Fn to jot: your words are typed into the app you are in. Click Record for meetings and long conversations. Everything lands in one library on your Mac. Glyph Record is now part of Glyph Jot.

Hold Fn to type

Hold Fn, say what you want, and let go. The words are typed wherever your cursor is: email, chat, documents, forms, the terminal, any app.

Fn + Shift for a note

Hold Fn + Shift instead and nothing is typed: the thought is saved as a note in your library.

Click Record for meetings

Zoom, Google Meet, FaceTime, Slack or Discord, or a talk in the room. It records your mic and the Mac's sound, with no bot joining the call, then writes the transcript, a summary and the action items.

Jot while you record

Hold Fn during a meeting and it types where your cursor is; that stretch is kept out of the meeting's transcript and summary. Fn + Shift saves a note linked to the meeting at that moment.

One library

Notes, dictations and recordings in one place, filed by People, Accounts and tags, so you find a call by who was there, not by date.

Cleans up as you talk

Ums, ahs and false starts are taken out, and "Tuesday, no wait, Thursday" types only Thursday. Formal in Mail, relaxed in Slack, exact in the terminal.

Learns your words

Fix a name or a term it got wrong, and it spells it right from then on. Add your own words in the Dictionary.

Ask what you said

"When is the launch now?" It answers from your own notes, dictations and meetings, on your Mac.

Private by design

Speech, transcripts and summaries run on your Mac with Apple's on-device models. No account, no audio sent anywhere, and it never types into a password field.

iPhone

The Glyph app and the Glyph keyboard on iPhone, in TestFlight testing and not on the App Store yet.

Needs

An Apple silicon Mac on macOS 26 or later. Free and open source on GitHub.
