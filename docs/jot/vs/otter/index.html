Glyph Jot vs Otter

Otter transcribes in its cloud and can send a bot into your calls. Glyph Jot records with no bot and keeps it all on your Mac.

Glyph JotFree
vs
Otter$16.99/mo

The short version

  • Otter Pro is $16.99 a month. Glyph Jot is free.
  • Otter trains on de-identified audio unless you turn it off. Jot sends nothing anywhere.
  • Otter also runs on Windows, the web and phones. Jot is Mac only, iPhone in testing.
