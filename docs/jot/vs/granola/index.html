Glyph Jot vs Granola

Granola has no bot either, but sends the audio to cloud services and needs an account. Glyph Jot keeps the whole meeting on your Mac.

vs
Granola$14/mo

The short version

  • Granola Business is $14 a user a month, and its free plan shows 30 days of history. Glyph Jot is free and keeps everything.
  • Neither one sends a bot into the call.
  • Granola transcribes in the cloud. Jot transcribes and summarizes on your Mac.
