Privacy

Glyph collects nothing. fru.dev runs no server for the apps and never sees your notes, recordings or photos.

On your device

Speech becomes text on your device with Apple's on-device models. Your memos, notes, recordings, photos and transcripts are kept on the device.

Your own vault

If you enter your own vault's address in Settings, your memos go there over your Tailscale network, and nowhere else.

Location, camera and microphone

Location, camera and microphone are used only when you allow them, for the memo you are making.

Nothing else

No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.

Contact

Questions go to an issue on the app's GitHub repository.
