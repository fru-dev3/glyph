01Is Glyph Cast free?

Yes, free and open source under the MIT license.

02Does it upload my recordings?

No. Transcription and cleanup run on your Mac, and recordings stay in your Movies folder as plain files.

03Does it work offline?

Yes, once the models (about 180 MB) have downloaded on the first recording.

04What does it export?

MP4 at 1080p, 1440p or 4K at 60 fps, with a matching .srt of the words you kept.

05Can I edit the words?

Yes. Click a word to play from there, Option-click to cut it or bring it back.

06What do I need?

An Apple silicon Mac on macOS 15 or later, and two free tools installed once with Homebrew: ffmpeg and whisper-cpp.

07Can you set it up for me?

Yes. One hour, live on your Mac, with the builder of Glyph: the apps, Jot dictation and meetings, and your AI agents on your own files. $149, and you pick the time right after paying.

08How can I support Glyph?

Every app stays free. If Glyph saves you a subscription, you can support it monthly or once at glyph.fru.dev/support. Nothing is locked.

Everything Glyph Cast does

A screen recorder first

Record any display with your mic and, if you want, the Mac's sound. Then Cast does the editing a recorder leaves to you.

Record from anywhere

Pick the display and microphone and press Option-Command-R. A countdown runs, and a bar at the top shows the time, your mic, Pause, Restart and Stop. Neither the bar nor the red edge is in the video.

Ready on its own

When you stop, it lines the mic up with the video, removes background noise, shapes and levels the voice, and transcribes it. About a minute for every ten recorded.

Ums and pauses

Filler words (um, uh, "the the", you know) and long pauses are found and shown in the transcript. One click removes them, a second click brings them back. Pauses while the screen was moving stay in.

Studio Sound

Noise removed, voice shaped (rumble cut, presence, de-ess, gentle compression) and leveled to -14 LUFS, YouTube's loudness. Blend your original mic back in if you like.

Edit the words

Click a word to play from there; Option-click to cut it or bring it back; select a stretch and Cut. The waveform shows every cut in red.

Framed and zoomed

Your screen on one of 18 wave wallpapers, your desktop picture or any image, with padding, corners and shadow, zooms where you click and click rings.

Intro and outro

Attach your own clips at export, or leave them off for this one.

Export MP4

1080p, 1440p or 4K at 60 fps, with a matching .srt of the words you kept.

Private

Transcription and cleanup run on your Mac. Recordings stay in your Movies folder as plain files.
