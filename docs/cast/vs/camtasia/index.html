Glyph Cast vs Camtasia

Camtasia is a full desktop editor sold by the year. Glyph Cast does the common cleanup on its own: the voice, the ums, the framing and the zooms.

The short version

  • Camtasia Essentials is $179.88 a year. Glyph Cast is free.
  • Both work on your computer and keep local files.
  • Camtasia is a full timeline editor. Cast cleans the recording up for you when you stop.
