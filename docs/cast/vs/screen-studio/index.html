Glyph Cast vs Screen Studio

Both record on your Mac and zoom in where you click. Screen Studio is a subscription; Glyph Cast is free and open source.

The short version

  • Screen Studio is $29 a month. Glyph Cast is free.
  • Both record and render on your Mac and work offline.
  • Cast also cleans the voice, finds ums and pauses, and writes an .srt.
