Glyph Cast vs Loom

Loom records straight to its cloud and shares a link. Glyph Cast records to a file on your Mac and cleans it up before you share.

Glyph CastFree
vs
Loom$18/mo

The short version

  • Loom Business is $18 a user a month, and its free plan stops at 5 minutes. Glyph Cast is free.
  • Loom needs the internet to record. Cast works offline.
  • Loom is made for a quick shared link. Cast gives you a finished MP4.
