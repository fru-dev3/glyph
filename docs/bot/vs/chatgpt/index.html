Glyph Bot vs ChatGPT

ChatGPT is OpenAI's hosted assistant: your chats live in your OpenAI account and run on OpenAI's models. Glyph Bot runs on the AI tools you already have and keeps chats and memory as plain files in your own vault.

Glyph BotFree app
vs
ChatGPT$20/mo

The short version

  • ChatGPT keeps chats on OpenAI's systems. Glyph Bot keeps them as files in your vault.
  • ChatGPT uses OpenAI's models. Glyph Bot uses Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI or local models.
  • ChatGPT trains on chats unless you opt out. Glyph Bot sends nothing to fru.dev.
