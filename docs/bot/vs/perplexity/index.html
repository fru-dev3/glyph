Glyph Bot vs Perplexity

Perplexity is a hosted answer engine that searches the web and cites what it finds. Glyph Bot cites too, but its sources are your own vault files and the Glyph apps' files, and the chats stay with you.

Glyph BotFree app
vs
Perplexity$17/mo

The short version

  • Perplexity cites the web. Glyph Bot cites your own files.
  • Perplexity keeps history on its service. Glyph Bot keeps it in your vault.
  • Perplexity uses data for training unless you turn off AI data retention.

When to pick Perplexity

Pick Perplexity when the answer is on the open web: current news, research with many citations, and apps on every platform.

When to pick Glyph Bot

You want your agents to work from your own files, cite them, and keep your chats in a vault you own.
