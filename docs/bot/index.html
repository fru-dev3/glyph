Everything Glyph Bot does

The agentic harness, and nothing else

Bot builds and runs the harness; the Rig connects and manages harnesses across machines.

Chat with your agents

Talk to your chief of staff and to one agent per domain.

Replies that show their sources

Before each reply it reads the sources that fit the question and hands the agent numbered excerpts. The reply cites them and lists them under "Read N sources, cited M".

Nothing saved until you say so

An agent asks to save; each request becomes a card with Save and Skip.

Your sources, with a switch each

Your vault and the Glyph apps' files in it (Well, Time, Jot, CRM, Omega), each with a switch for whether agents may read it.
